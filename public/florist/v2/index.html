About the Artist

Florist wasn't born in a studio. It started with a handful of tiles, a pair of cutters, and hours on YouTube figuring out how to make something that would last.

The practice has since evolved into fine art and collectible works. Florist now focuses on original pieces, limited editions and one-off commissions for collectors and galleries.
