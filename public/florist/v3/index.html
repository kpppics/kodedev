05 / About

Florist.

Florist wasn't born in a studio. It started with a handful of tiles, a pair of cutters, and hours on YouTube figuring out how to make something that would last.

Experiments with random bits of pixel art turned into the Florist pattern, a tight, repeating design that feels like order carved out of chaos. Now I look for the places the city leaves blank, boarded-up windows, bricked-in doorways, forgotten walls. The spaces that would otherwise stay empty.

Forty four installations later, the work has moved indoors too: originals, limited editions and one-off commissions.
