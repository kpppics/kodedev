About Florist

Florist is a London mosaic artist working in hand-cut tile, turning a pixel-simple visual language into pieces installed on walls across the city and, increasingly, framed for the wall at home.

What began on the street has become a body of collectible work: originals, editions and one-off commissions. This is the archive, the shop and the record of where the work has gone.
