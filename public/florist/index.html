Statement

A pixel-simple language, cut by hand into something you can own.

What began as installations on the walls of East London has grown into a studio practice: original mosaic works, framed editions and one-off commissions. The same bold, blocky alphabet, now made to be lived with, and increasingly, to be collected.

About the artist

Florist

Florist is a London mosaic artist working in hand-cut tile. What started as street installations has become a considered studio practice, producing original works and limited editions for collectors and galleries.

The studio is developing new work toward gallery representation, and welcomes enquiries from collectors and curators.
