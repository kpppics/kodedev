Saving and printing

Save image downloads the full 1200 × 1800 file. On an iPhone it lands in Files. To get it into Photos instead, press and hold the picture and choose Add to Photos.

Print 4×6 opens a page sized to exactly 4 × 6 inches and brings up the print dialog. Pick your 4 × 6 paper and make sure scaling is set to 100% and not Fit to page.

How the live capture would work

  1. Shoot the fan or group on the phone outside the ground.
  2. Pick a design and type their name.
  3. Card builds with the photo, name, fixture and date dropped in.
  4. Hand it over: QR or AirDrop to their phone, and a 4 × 6 to a pocket printer.

The fan in these previews is a stand-in so you can judge the layouts. The crest, cannon and kit are the real Arsenal marks, as you asked. Worth revisiting before anything is sold at volume, but fine for now.
