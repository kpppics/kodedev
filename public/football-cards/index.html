Printer settings that matter

Print at 100% or Actual size. If it is set to Fit to page the cards come out undersized and the crop marks stop lining up. Cut on the corner ticks, then glue the two halves back to back for a proper double-sided card.

How the live capture would work

  1. Shoot the fan or group on the phone outside the ground.
  2. Cut out the subject from the background automatically.
  3. Drop them onto the chosen template. Photo, name, fixture and date all fill in, front and back together.
  4. Deliver: QR or AirDrop straight to their phone, and a print-ready 4 × 6 to a pocket printer.

The fan in these previews is a stand-in so you can judge the layouts. The crest and cannon are placeholder marks. Club badges are trademarked, so anything sold or printed at volume needs your own mark or permission.
